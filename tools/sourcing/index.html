Purpose

Tech sourcing fails when teams start with a blank search bar and a vague brief. Sourcing A[i]gent exists to make “where to look and what to look for” explicit — so sourcing becomes teachable, repeatable, and measurable.

Faster time-to-first-outreach Turn intake into ready-to-run patterns and target lists in minutes, not days.
Shared sourcing truth Replace personal bookmarks with reusable templates the whole TA team can use.
Clean handoffs Structured outputs that feed Screening + Metrics so pipeline health is debuggable.

What breaks without this

  • Every req starts from scratch — patterns live in individual memory, not the system.
  • Coverage is inconsistent: hidden pockets of candidates never get searched.
  • Hiring managers can’t see (or trust) where you’re targeting — only that “we’re sourcing.”
  • Sourcing effort is hard to measure and harder to debug when pipelines dry up.
  • Downstream stages get noisy because upstream inputs weren’t structured or calibrated.

What it does

  • Translates intake into a structured role profile (titles, stacks, must-haves, negatives).
  • Generates patterns for LinkedIn, GitHub, and open-web X-ray search — transparent and editable.
  • Builds a market map (Tier 1 competitors → Tier 2 adjacencies → Tier 3 hidden gems).
  • Surfaces readiness signals (pool tightness, competition pressure, speed sensitivity).
  • Creates clean handoffs into Screening and Metrics so sourcing ROI becomes visible.

Note: Sourcing A[i]gent is not an auto-outreach system. It produces explainable inputs humans can approve, run, and improve.

Where it fits

  • Upstream of Screening: role templates inform rubrics and “what good looks like.”
  • Upstream of Metrics: pattern IDs + channel plans enable attribution and funnel diagnostics.
  • Feeds Chief: market signals become escalation inputs (tight pool, high competition, comp risk).
