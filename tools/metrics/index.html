What this tool is

Pipeline Health is the diagnostics layer for recruiting execution. It reads funnel counts and stage movement (from ATS + agent-emitted events), flags abnormal patterns, and turns them into operator-facing actions — not dashboard noise.

What breaks without it

  • Funnel performance is debated instead of understood.
  • “Actionable” dashboards create noise, not decisions.
  • Bottlenecks are discovered late, after damage is done.
  • KPI truth gets hidden in performative actions instead of operational reality.
  • Metrics lose credibility and trust erodes — which slows execution everywhere.

What it does

  • Diagnoses pass-through drop-offs by stage and flags abnormal patterns.
  • Detects constraints (capacity, volume, timing mismatches) before teams feel them.
  • Surfaces likely causes and recommended next actions (operator-facing, not analytics-speak).
  • Executes Tier-1 actions automatically when pre-approved (alerts, nudges, agenda updates).
Outcome: Faster execution. Cleaner decisions. Fewer stalled roles.

Where it fits

Pipeline Health sits between raw execution data and governed action. It makes drift visible early and gives Chief consistent thresholds for autonomy and escalation.

Signal Stage counts, time-in-stage, pass-through, SLAs, capacity
Diagnosis Explainable bottlenecks and alerts with likely drivers
Action Tier-1 nudges + escalations to Chief when thresholds trip

How to use it

  1. Open the demo to see how stage inputs generate diagnoses.
  2. Use the playbook to standardize definitions and dashboards.
  3. Wire tool events into Chief for governance + escalation.
