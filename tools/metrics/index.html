Purpose

Hiring decisions fail when teams don’t trust or understand the numbers. Pipeline Health exists to remove ambiguity and make funnel performance explainable — not debatable.

What breaks without this

  • Funnel performance is debated instead of understood.
  • “Actionable” dashboards create noise, not decisions.
  • Bottlenecks are discovered late, after damage is done.
  • KPI truth gets hidden in performative actions instead of operational reality.
  • Metrics lose credibility and trust erodes — which slows execution everywhere.

What it does

  • Diagnoses pass-through drop-offs by stage (and flags abnormal patterns).
  • Detects constraints (capacity, volume, timing mismatches) before teams feel them.
  • Surfaces likely causes and recommended next actions (operator-facing, not “analytics speak”).
  • Executes Tier-1 actions automatically when pre-approved (agenda updates, alerts, nudges).

Note: Pipeline Health is not “the guardrail layer.” It enforces honesty across the system — guardrails live in the playbooks.

Where it fits

  • Downstream of Hiring & Mobility signals: interviews, offers, time-in-stage, pass-through, SLAs.
  • Upstream of Planning, Innovation, and Governance: makes drift and decay visible early.
  • Feeds Chief A[i]gent: thresholds for autonomy and escalation signals.
