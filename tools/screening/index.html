Why it matters

  • Speed: shrink review time (e.g., 15 minutes → ~60–90 seconds per resume).
  • Consistency: the same intel is captured every time, which reduces drift and bias.
  • Quality: more “rescued” candidates surface because the gray zone gets real attention.
  • Governance: human overrides require notes — creating an audit trail.

What it does

  • Transforms JDs + rules into rubrics.
  • Scores soft skills signals from real evidence (comms, detail, follow-through).
  • Verifies must-haves, education, years, and average tenure.
  • Maps employer history to competitor/adjacent categories.
  • Suggests ask-next questions for flags.
  • Recommends Advance / HM-Review / Hold with an explanation.
