Purpose

Hiring plans fail when teams don’t connect targets to capacity and timing. This engine exists to make hiring math explicit: what volume you need, what workload you’re creating, and which constraint will break first.

Forecastable execution Turn conversion rates + time lags into a weekly plan your team can actually run.
Capacity truth Expose interview load and recruiter bandwidth before reqs stall or quality drops.
Earlier risk signals See time-to-fill and bottlenecks early enough to change course (source mix, bar, process).

What breaks without this

  • Leaders set hiring targets without understanding the interview load they’re committing to.
  • Recruiters are held accountable for time-to-fill without controlling capacity constraints.
  • Conversion-rate assumptions drift silently until the quarter is already lost.
  • “More sourcing” becomes the default fix even when the real constraint is downstream (HM bar, panels, offer timing).
  • Teams burn out because the weekly operating plan is implicit instead of explicit.

What it does

  • Back-solves required volume at each stage to hit a hires target.
  • Forecasts time-to-fill using stage lags (screen → panel, offer → accept, accept → start).
  • Creates a weekly plan (screens, HM interviews, finals, offers) aligned to capacity.
  • Shows the constraint (volume, conversion, timing, or interviewer load) so fixes are targeted.

Note: This engine is intentionally transparent—inputs are editable and assumptions are visible so operators can calibrate in real time.

Where it fits

  • Upstream of execution: turns hiring targets into a weekly operating plan.
  • Alongside Pipeline Health: forecasting sets the plan; diagnostics explain drift and recommend fixes.
  • Feeds Chief A[i]gent: provides the “expected” baselines and thresholds for alerts and autonomy.
