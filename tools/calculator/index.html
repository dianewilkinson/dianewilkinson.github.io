Purpose

Recruiting plans fail when teams plan the outcome (hires) but don’t plan the math (weekly throughput, conversion rates, and lead time). This engine makes constraints visible early — so targets and resourcing can be corrected before the quarter is lost.

Forecast volume Convert hires into required weekly candidates per stage using your CVRs.
Expose constraints See which stage breaks first (screens, HM capacity, finals, offer cycle).
Weekly execution plan Outputs a week-by-week planner that turns a forecast into action.

What breaks without this

  • Time-to-fill becomes a hope, not a plan.
  • Hiring managers commit to headcount without understanding interview capacity.
  • Recruiters get blamed for outcomes that were mathematically impossible.
  • Process improvements are random because constraints aren’t isolated.

What it does

  • Inputs: start date, hiring goal, lead times, admin time, and stage conversion rates.
  • Outputs: active interview weeks, time-to-fill estimate, weekly volume by stage, and a planner grid.
  • Scenarios: adjust CVRs or time assumptions and immediately see the new plan.
