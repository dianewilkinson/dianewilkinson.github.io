Purpose

Most org “listening” fails because signals are unstructured, anonymous, and disconnected from action. Listening A[i]gent creates a consistent signal layer that preserves context and routes feedback into visible decisions and closed-loop change.

Early warning Surface repeat friction and risk patterns before they become attrition, escalation, or performance issues.
Trustworthy insight Preserve attribution and context so leaders can act with confidence—not debate survey scores.
Closed loop Turn feedback into owned actions and visible “what changed” follow-through.

What breaks without this

  • “Listening” becomes low-trust surveys with low response rates and unclear meaning.
  • Anonymous comments create defensiveness with no clear ownership or follow-through.
  • Signals stay trapped in docs, DMs, and 1:1 notes—no consistent taxonomy or audit trail.
  • Leaders see dashboards but can’t act because insight isn’t tied to a decision owner.
  • Feedback never closes the loop, so trust erodes and the same issues repeat.

What it does

  • Captures signals from existing artifacts (notes, retros, feedback, handoffs) without adding process burden.
  • Structures qualitative input into a taxonomy (theme, risk, severity, confidence, owner, status).
  • Routes signals into a human triage loop with recommended owners and next steps.
  • Closes the loop by tracking progress: captured → triaged → actioned → communicated.

Note: Guardrail-first by design—no AI scoring for employee performance decisions; sensitive signals are access-controlled and auditable.

Where it fits

  • Downstream of Listening & Insights: leadership decisions, Ops prioritization, and change planning with visible follow-through.
  • Upstream of: Innovation experiments and the Metrics layer (signals become measurable progress).
  • Feeds Innovation and Metrics: friction themes become experiment candidates; closed-loop action becomes trackable outcomes.
