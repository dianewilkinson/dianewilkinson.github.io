Listening A[i]gent Demo Coming Soon.

This demo will show how raw feedback becomes structured signals (theme, risk, confidence, owner, status) — and how the loop closes: captured → triaged → actioned → communicated.

What the demo will include

Signal capture Example inputs from notes, retros, and feedback — with attribution preserved.
Structured taxonomy Theme + risk + confidence + owner + status, normalized for reporting and action.
Closed-loop tracking Routing, ownership, and “what changed” visibility — not anonymous dashboards.

Note: Guardrail-first by design — no employee performance scoring; access-controlled sensitive signals; auditable change history.
