How I Work

I build practical systems leaders can trust: workflow-first, evidence-first, human judgment preserved, and decisions made auditable — with guardrails on AI autonomy.

Operating principles

  • Workflow-first: reduce friction before adding “AI magic.”
  • Evidence-first: show what drove the insight (“why this surfaced”).
  • Human-in-the-loop: AI assists; humans decide when consequences exist.
  • Auditability: actions and rationale are logged for review.
  • Trust-building: design for adoption and reduced defensiveness.

Read: AI-First Ops Model

Guardrails on AI Autonomy

Tier 0 — Monitor Baseline + drift detection. No action, no noise.
Tier 1 — Auto-execute (low risk) Reminders, routing, refreshes, nudges, task creation. Logged and visible.
Tier 2 — Recommend (approval required) Actions that affect people or process outcomes require a human decision.
Tier 3 — Escalate (high risk) Employment-impacting decisions are never automated; multi-stakeholder governance applies.
