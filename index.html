Guardrails on Autonomy

What can safely auto-execute, what requires approval, and what must always escalate — with auditability built in.

Guardrails on AI Autonomy

Tier 0 — Monitor Baseline + drift detection. No action, no noise.
Tier 1 — Auto-execute (low risk) Reminders, routing, refreshes, nudges. Logged and visible.
Tier 2 — Recommend (approval required) Actions affecting outcomes require a human decision.
Tier 3 — Escalate (high risk) Employment-impacting decisions are never automated.
