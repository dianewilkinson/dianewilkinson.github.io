AI-Native People Ops System

This system shows how I design internal, workflow-first AI across People Operations — with shared signals, governed decision logic, and auditability built in.

System Overview

The People Ops System is organized around four lifecycle stages. Each stage uses the same underlying architecture for signals, decision logic, and governance — avoiding tool sprawl and brittle handoffs.

🎯 Hire

  • Funnel health & throughput diagnostics
  • Capacity-aware forecasting
  • Evidence-based screening support

🦾 Support

  • Workflow automation & routing
  • Operational visibility (SLAs, queues)
  • Manager-safe systems

📊 Evaluate

  • Listening & lifecycle signals
  • Open-text synthesis
  • Shared metric definitions

🏅 Improve

  • Quality loops & experimentation
  • Governed system changes
  • Compounding process improvements

Governance & Safety

AI is assistive by default. Autonomy is explicitly tiered to protect people, decisions, and organizational trust.

Guardrails on AI Autonomy

Tier 0 — Monitor Baseline measurement and drift detection only.
Tier 1 — Auto-execute (low risk) Reminders, routing, refreshes, and task creation. Logged and visible.
Tier 2 — Recommend Actions affecting outcomes require explicit human approval.
Tier 3 — Escalate Employment-impacting decisions are never automated.
