Innovation A[i]gent — Structured Improvement Without Chaos

Turning ideas into tested changes — not endless pilots.

Purpose:
Innovation A[i]gent creates a lightweight, evidence-based system for testing improvements in people workflows — without “innovation theater” or tool sprawl.

1. The Problem

Most innovation efforts in people systems fail because they are:

  • Detached from real operational pain
  • Run as one-off pilots
  • Unmeasured or over-measured
  • Forgotten when priorities shift

2. What the Innovation A[i]gent Does

  • Captures improvement ideas tied to real workflows
  • Frames ideas as testable hypotheses
  • Defines success metrics before launch
  • Tracks outcomes and decisions

3. Inputs

  • Insights from Listening A[i]gent
  • Metrics anomalies and bottlenecks
  • Operator suggestions (recruiters, ops, managers)
  • Post-mortems and retrospectives

4. Experiment Design Principles

  • Small, reversible changes first
  • One variable per test where possible
  • Time-boxed experiments
  • Pre-defined success and stop criteria

5. Outputs

  • Documented experiments and learnings
  • Adopt / iterate / retire decisions
  • Reusable patterns and playbooks
  • Clear ownership of changes

6. Where It Fits in the A[i]gent Suite

  • Consumes signals from Listening and Metrics A[i]gents
  • Feeds improvements into Workflow A[i]gent
  • Informs Enablement and Training updates

7. What This Is Not

  • Not an idea backlog graveyard
  • Not a hackathon replacement
  • Not AI deciding what to change

Innovation works when it’s operational, owned, and measurable.
