A[i]gent Suite – People Systems Overview

Internal · Evidence-First · Human-in-the-Loop · Auditable Workflows

A[i]gent Suite

A modular suite of internal people systems agents that work together across HR workflows.

These A[i]gents are designed to live inside the tools teams already use (ATS, HRIS, knowledge bases) — routing the right evidence to the right decision at the right moment.

🧭 One operating model: Hire · Support · Evaluate · Improve

← Back to Portfolio

Suite at a glance

  • Built for auditability: evidence, controls, and human override.
  • Designed to reduce friction without “ripping and replacing” systems.
  • Extends beyond recruiting: knowledge, performance, listening, capability planning.
  • Metrics + Capabilities form the foundation layer.
This page is the overview. Deep dives live in each agent’s demo & playbook.
