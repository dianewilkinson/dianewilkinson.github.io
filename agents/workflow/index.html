What Workflow A[i]gent solves

Operational drag
Less chasing, more recruiting. Automates the repetitive communication so recruiters spend time on calibration, decision support, and candidate coaching instead of reminders and manual updates.
Candidate experience
No more “black box” stages. Every step has a clear expectation, confirmation, and follow-up, with delay alerts routed to recruiters whenever signals indicate risk or confusion.
Funnel health
Tighter feedback loops. Scorecard nudges and interview SLAs keep decisions moving, reduce idle time in the pipeline, and give downstream agents (like Metrics A[i]gent) better signal quality.
