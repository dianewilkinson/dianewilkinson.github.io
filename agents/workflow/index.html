Initial Screen workflow
Goal: convert interest into booked time

When a candidate moves into the screening stage, Workflow A[i]gent sequences availability, confirmation, and reminder flows to minimize drop-off before the first conversation.

  • Stage change → availability request Candidate email · Immediate
  • No reply in 48 hours → gentle nudge Candidate email · +48h
  • No reply in 96 hours → recruiter alert Recruiter alert · +96h
  • Event booked → calendar invite + confirmation Calendar + template
DW
Sample: Initial screen confirmation
Candidate-facing
Subject
[Company] – Initial Conversation for [Role] – [Date/Time]
Preview
Thanks for taking the time to speak with us! This 30-minute call will focus on your experience, what you’re looking for next, and how this role fits. If you need to reschedule or request accommodations, you can use the link below or reply directly to this email…
Automated on event booking Backstopped by recruiter if anything changes.
HM / Technical interview workflow
Goal: keep candidates & interviewers aligned

Once a candidate is through initial fit, Workflow A[i]gent supports deeper interviews with structured prep, interviewer reminders, and scorecard SLAs.

  • Event booked → confirmation + prep Candidate email · Immediate
  • Upcoming interview → candidate reminder Candidate email · -24h
  • Upcoming interview → interviewer reminder Interviewer email · -2h
  • Interview complete → scorecard nudge Interviewer email · +2h / +24h
DW
Sample: HM interview prep email
Candidate-facing
Subject
[Company] – HM/Technical Interview for [Role] – What to Expect
Preview
In this conversation we’ll go deeper into how you approach your work, walk through a few recent projects, and leave time for your questions. You do not need to prepare a formal presentation. If you prefer a different format or need accommodations, reply and we’ll adjust…
Paired with interviewer scorecard nudges Feedback stays fresh and decisions move quickly.
Panel interview workflow
Goal: coordinate multiple stakeholders

For panel stages, Workflow A[i]gent ensures candidates know the structure and interviewers know their focus area, while maintaining tight feedback loops.

  • Panel confirmed → consolidated agenda Candidate email · Immediate
  • Panel upcoming → group reminder Candidate & interviewers · -24h
  • Panel complete → scorecard nudges per interviewer Interviewer emails · +2h / +24h
  • Missing feedback → HM escalation HM / Lead · +48h
DW
Sample: Panel agenda overview
Candidate-facing
Preview
Your panel will be three 30-minute conversations focused on: (1) how you collaborate across teams, (2) how you structure ambiguous projects, and (3) how you think about impact. Each interviewer has a different lens, but you don’t need separate prep for each block…
Panel expectations are clear Reduces candidate anxiety and improves signal quality.
Final interview workflow
Goal: de-risk closing conversations

Final conversations are highly sensitive. Workflow A[i]gent focuses on clarity, expectations, and timely follow-up so high-intent candidates don’t stall out.

  • Final booked → confirmation + expectations Candidate email · Immediate
  • Upcoming → reminder + interviewer brief Candidate & HM · -24h
  • Final complete → “what to expect next” Candidate email · +2h
  • Decision delayed → status update Candidate email · +3–7 days
DW
Sample: Final stage expectations
Candidate-facing
Preview
After this conversation, our team reviews feedback and aligns on next steps. In most cases you’ll hear from us within 3–5 business days. If anything changes with that timing, we’ll proactively reach out so you’re not left wondering.
Delay updates are built in Protects candidate experience at the most critical point.
Offer workflow
Goal: structured, human closing

Offers are still delivered by humans. Workflow A[i]gent supports them with structured timing, expectations, and follow-through so candidates feel guided, not pressured.

  • Offer approved → schedule offer conversation Candidate email · Immediate
  • Offer call complete → written offer summary Candidate email · Same day
  • Decision window → check-in & Q&A Candidate email · +2–5 days
  • Offer accepted → “what happens next” New hire email · Immediate
DW
Sample: Offer follow-up
Candidate-facing
Preview
It was great speaking with you today. Attached is a written summary of your offer details, including base, bonus, equity, and benefits. Take the time you need to review; if helpful we’re happy to set up another call to walk through questions or talk about start dates.
Clear, calm closing motion Structured without feeling transactional.
Pre-start workflow
Goal: keep accepted offers warm

Post-accept, Workflow A[i]gent makes sure new hires know what to expect between now and day one, reducing anxiety and minimizing risk of last-minute fall-off.

  • Offer accepted → welcome + next steps New hire email · Immediate
  • Paperwork window → reminders as needed New hire email · Configurable
  • 1 week before start → first day logistics New hire email · -7 days
  • Day before start → brief welcome note New hire email · -1 day
DW
Sample: Pre-start welcome
New hire-facing
Preview
We’re excited to have you join! Between now and your first day you’ll receive a few brief notes about benefits, equipment, and logistics. On day one, you’ll start with onboarding and team introductions before diving into role-specific work.
Closes the loop after offer New hires know what’s coming next.
