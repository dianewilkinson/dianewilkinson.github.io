Listening A[i]gent — Organizational Signal & Feedback

Turning qualitative noise into structured insight — without surveys nobody trusts.

Purpose:
Listening A[i]gent captures, organizes, and contextualizes feedback signals already present across people systems — without introducing performative listening or black-box sentiment scores.

1. The Problem

Most organizations believe they are “listening,” but rely on:

  • Low-response engagement surveys
  • Anonymous comments with no ownership
  • One-off feedback that never loops back
  • Sentiment dashboards divorced from decisions

The result is signal loss, trust erosion, and leadership blind spots.

2. What the Listening A[i]gent Does

  • Collects feedback signals from existing workflows (interviews, check-ins, retros, notes)
  • Structures qualitative input into themes, risks, and trends
  • Preserves attribution and context where appropriate
  • Surfaces insights alongside quantitative metrics

3. Inputs (Non-Invasive by Design)

  • Interview feedback and scorecard comments
  • Recruiter and manager notes
  • Candidate experience feedback
  • Exit or transition summaries
  • Operational retrospectives

4. Human-in-the-Loop Guardrails

  • No automated sentiment scoring without context
  • No anonymous aggregation used for performance management
  • Human review required before insights are elevated
  • Clear distinction between signal, hypothesis, and conclusion

5. Outputs

  • Thematic summaries tied to specific workflows
  • Early warning indicators (friction, burnout, confusion)
  • Decision-ready insights for Ops and leadership
  • Documented feedback loops showing “what changed”

6. Where It Fits in the A[i]gent Suite

  • Pairs with Metrics A[i]gent to explain *why* numbers move
  • Feeds Innovation A[i]gent with grounded problem statements
  • Supports Enablement, Coaching, and Quality agents

7. Risks & Mitigations

  • Risk: Over-indexing on loud voices
    Mitigation: Pattern validation across multiple sources
  • Risk: Feedback without action
    Mitigation: Required follow-up linkage

Listening is not about collecting more data — it’s about closing loops.
